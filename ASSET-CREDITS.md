Modifications for Iron Arena: selected animation clips retargeted by bone name/rest rotation, original bone lengths retained, hairstyles merged onto each skeleton, textures resized (1024 maximum for body, 512 for hair/eyes) and embedded as JPEG, packed as self-contained GLB. Combat outfits are rendered using a custom material. Kick is a procedural animation, not a Quaternius clip. Character display names Kai and Aya are assigned by this game.
